# Policies and support

## Late work and extensions

Assignments submitted after the deadline lose 5% of the available mark per
day, up to five days, after which they are not accepted without an approved
extension. Extensions are granted for documented illness, carer responsibility,
or equivalent circumstances — email the convenor before the deadline where
possible, and as soon as you're able to otherwise.

## Academic integrity

Cite every source you draw a claim, figure, or dataset from, including the
national collision dataset used throughout the unit. Discussing ideas with
classmates is expected; submitting someone else's analysis or write-up as your
own is not. If you're unsure whether a particular use of a source or a tool
crosses a line, ask before submitting.

## Getting help

Bring content and research-design questions to sessions — that's what they're
for. Use email for anything that can't wait until the next one: enrolment
questions and assessment logistics to Marisol Quaye, and week-to-week material
to either convenor or the tutor. See the [People](/people/) page for contact
details.
