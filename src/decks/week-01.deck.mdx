# SLOP8258

## Vehicular Macropod Avoidance

---

{/* _class: impact */}

# ~16,000

reported kangaroo–vehicle collisions in Australia every year — and that's
before you adjust for underreporting

---

## Why this unit exists

- a real, recurring safety problem with a genuine research literature behind
  it
- touches ecology, physics, human factors, engineering, and policy at once
- no single discipline's account of it is sufficient on its own

---

## How the semester runs

1. **weeks 1–5** — ecology, the dataset, mapping, impact mechanics, human
   factors
2. **weeks 6–7** — technology and road engineering
3. **weeks 8–10** — law, procedure, and risk communication
4. **weeks 11–12** — comparative literature and synthesis

---

## The two pieces of assessed work

- **week 6** — literature review and hotspot analysis (40%)
- **week 12** — research proposal for a safety intervention (60%)

Both are research artefacts: a reader who wasn't in the room should be able
to follow the argument and trust the evidence behind it.
