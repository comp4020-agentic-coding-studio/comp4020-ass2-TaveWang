## What "crepuscular" actually means

- most active around twilight — not fixed to a clock time
- a real, evidenced tendency across a population
- **not** a complete explanation for a specific spike in a specific dataset

> Crepuscular is a behavioural pattern, not a booking confirmation for
> 6:00pm.

---

## Why kangaroos approach roads

- food and water access on disturbed roadside verges
- habitat fragmentation — the road cuts across an existing range
- established movement routes between shelter and grazing
- seasonal conditions: drought range shifts, joey season
- mobs move together

---

## Animal activity, vehicle exposure and reporting

- driver visibility drops as light falls; glare is worst near dawn and dusk
- traffic volume peaks at the same hours — commute time
- roadside vegetation can block the sightline early

> Observed collision records = animal presence × vehicle exposure ×
> probability of collision × probability of reporting

---

## An inconveniently exact appointment

- a disproportionate number of records sit at exactly **06:00** or
  exactly **18:00**
- almost all from one `report_source`: wildlife carer
- `data_quality_flag`: shift-start time recorded, not incident time

> Either the kangaroos became remarkably punctual, or a human system has
> entered the graph.

---

## Pattern versus explanation

| Observed pattern | Tempting explanation | Alternatives |
| --- | --- | --- |
| more dusk records | more animal activity | traffic, visibility, reporting |
| more records on one road | greater collision risk | traffic volume, reporting coverage |

---

## The revised conclusion

> The synthetic dataset contains more dusk records, but some timestamps
> reflect wildlife-carer shift times rather than incident times. The
> remaining pattern is consistent with several explanations, including
> animal activity, traffic exposure and visibility, which cannot be
> separated using these records alone.

A pattern in the spreadsheet may describe kangaroos — or it may describe
the person holding the clipboard.
