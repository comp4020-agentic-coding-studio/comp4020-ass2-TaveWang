# Week 7

## Fencing, signage and wildlife crossings

### The Best Result In The Literature Is Also The Hardest One To Fund

---

## Four mechanisms

1. **exclude** — fencing
2. **redirect** — a safer crossing (underpass, culvert)
3. **change driver behaviour** — signage, dynamic warnings
4. **change road-use conditions** — sightlines, lighting, speed

Most measures you'll compare today combine 1 and 2.

```notes
Ask the room which of the four a static warning sign belongs to before
moving on — it's category 3, and it's the weakest one.
```

---

## Fencing works until it doesn't

A fence that ends doesn't remove animals from the area — it relocates where
they cross: to the end of the fence.

This is a real displacement effect, and a source of measurement noise in
the studies you're about to read.

---

## Usage ≠ collisions

A well-used underpass is evidence the structure works **as a crossing**.

It is not, on its own, evidence that road collisions went down.

Two different measurements. Don't recommend on the wrong one.

## Why "install everything" isn't a recommendation

- competes for the same limited budget
- competes for the same finite approval bandwidth
- a list of every measure is a refusal to choose

One primary measure, argued for one site. That's the shape Week 12 will ask
for again, at the scale of the whole proposal.
