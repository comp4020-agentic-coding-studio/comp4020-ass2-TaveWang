## Before the session

Bring your week 2 time distribution. Skim the lecture's account of spatial
clustering before you arrive.

## In the session

Plot strikes against the regional road network and mark candidate hotspots.
Swap maps with another group and try to break their definition — a stretch
of road with high traffic volume but low strike density is the usual trap.

## Afterwards

Your revised hotspot definition, and the map it produces, is the raw material
for the week 6 assignment.
