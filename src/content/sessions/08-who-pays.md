## Before the session

Read the three fictional cases (A, B and C) and the property-damage /
personal-injury table in this week's lecture.

## In the session — "The Invoice Allocation Hearing"

| Step | Time | Activity |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 5 min | Recap the NSW s45 actual-knowledge standard and the property/injury table as a group. |
| 2 | 10 min | Small groups are each assigned one case (A, B or C) and determine who plausibly pays, citing the specific clause, scheme or legal standard that applies. |
| 3 | 10 min | Each group presents its reasoning to a group that worked a different case. |
| 4 | 10 min | Cross-examine: the listening group asks what additional evidence would change the presenting group's answer. |
| 5 | 10 min | Cases are reassigned with the new complication raised in cross-examination added; groups re-answer quickly. |
| 6 | 5 min | Write the **Invoice Allocation Memo** (see below). |

### Invoice Allocation Memo (formative, not a final-project attachment)

One paragraph per case (A, B, C): who plausibly pays, the specific basis
(clause, scheme, or standard), and your confidence in that answer given what
evidence exists. This is a formative output for this week's activity only.

## Afterwards

Keep your notes on what evidence each case actually turned on — that
distinction between *what happened* and *what could be proven to have
happened* is exactly what Week 9's reporting-obligations material builds on.
