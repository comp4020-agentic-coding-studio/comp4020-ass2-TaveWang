## Before the session

Read the lecture's material on glare and dusk contrast before you arrive; the
exercise assumes you already know why headlight glare is a separate problem
from low light.

## In the session

Timed detection trials against recorded dusk-driving footage, run twice —
once alert, once after a short fatiguing task — to isolate what fatigue
changes versus what low light already explains.

## Afterwards

Your two detection-time results feed directly into the literature review due
next week: they're your own data point against the published reaction-time
figures.
