## In the session — "The Proposal Defence Clinic"

A fictional review panel of Drs Quaye, Fenn, and Okafor sits in on this
session in spirit — the feedback prompts below are the questions they
would ask. For cohorts larger than roughly 12, run this in parallel small
groups rather than one whole-class queue, so every proposal gets a real
defence round within the time available.

| Step | Time | Activity |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 5 min | Recap the six canvas fields and what a marker checks for at each. |
| 2 | 30 min | Small-group defence rounds: each student walks their group through their canvas in about 3 minutes, using the feedback prompts below. |
| 3 | 10 min | Peer feedback: for each proposal, answer the four prompt questions and note the answers for the presenter. |
| 4 | 5 min | Submit the **Proposal Survival Checklist** (see below). |

### Feedback prompt questions

1. What's the single intervention?
2. Which three strands does it argue from?
3. What's the realistic cost and approval pathway?
4. What would count as evidence it worked?

### Proposal Survival Checklist (formative)

A short checklist noting which of the six canvas fields are solid, which
need more work, and the single most useful piece of feedback you received.
This is a formative output for this week's activity — it adds no separate
grade of its own.

## Afterwards

This session and its feedback are formative only. Revise your written
proposal based on what came up here, and submit it via the [final
project](/assessments/final-project/) by the real deadline — 28 May 2027,
12:00 — which this session does not change or extend.
