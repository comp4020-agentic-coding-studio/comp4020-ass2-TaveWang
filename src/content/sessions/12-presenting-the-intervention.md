## Before the session

Have a presentable draft of your proposal, even if the written version isn't
finished.

## In the session

Five-minute presentations followed by questions from the group — treat the
questions as a dry run for anything a marker might ask of the written
version.

## Afterwards

Revise the written proposal in light of the questions before submitting it.
