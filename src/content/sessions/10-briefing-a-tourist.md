## Before the session

Draft a one-page risk briefing aimed at a first-time visitor picking up a
rental car.

## In the session

Swap drafts with a partner who reads it cold and flags anything confusing,
alarmist, or missing. Revise on the spot.

## Afterwards

Keep the revised briefing — it's a candidate artefact for the final project's
intervention.
