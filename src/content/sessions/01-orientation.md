## Before the session

Read the syllabus and the two papers linked from the opening lecture. Bring
one popular-media claim about kangaroo-vehicle collisions you've encountered —
a news story, a road sign, a rental-car pamphlet — that you suspect oversimplifies.

## In the session

A short framing talk, then time in groups auditing the shared dataset: what it
records, what it can't tell you, and where its gaps are going to bite later in
the semester.

## Afterwards

You leave with dataset access, a partner for the week 3 mapping exercise, and
the one claim you brought marked up with what evidence would settle it.
