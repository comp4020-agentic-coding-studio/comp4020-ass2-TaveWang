## Before the session

Read the two comparative case studies linked from the lecture.

## In the session

In groups, argue for or against transferring a specific overseas intervention
to the Australian context, using the dataset and case studies as evidence
rather than intuition.

## Afterwards

Your transfer argument, whichever side you took, is useful material for
justifying design choices in the final project.
