## Before the session

Bring a calculator and this week's lecture notes on impact biomechanics. No
prior physics background is assumed.

## In the session

Work through impact-energy estimates for a small set of real recorded speeds
and animal masses, then discuss where the numbers agree with driver instinct
and where they don't.

## Afterwards

Keep your worked estimates — the week 6 assignment asks you to apply the same
method to a hotspot you haven't seen before.
