Callum spent three years running roadside trials for wildlife-detection
sensors, watching vendor demos work perfectly in daylight and fall apart at
dusk. He teaches the weeks where the promising pilot study meets the
messier business of a highway at night.

He's the one who'll ask what the false-negative rate was before he asks
what the accuracy was.
