Dr Kargaroo joined the unit as a co-convenor shortly after a collision on the
Monaro Highway gave him what the rest of the teaching team politely calls "an
unusually direct research method." He is, as far as the department is aware,
the only member of staff who is also a primary data point in the dataset.

He teaches from experience rather than from the literature, and is candid
that the two don't always agree. Consultation hours are whenever he's hopping
past the office.
