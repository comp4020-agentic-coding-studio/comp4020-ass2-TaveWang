Marisol set up the unit after five years collecting roadside collision data
across regional New South Wales, and still argues most of it never gets read
past the executive summary. She teaches the weeks that ask you to make a claim
and then check it against a dataset.

Consultation hours are posted on the unit's noticeboard each fortnight; email
is for anything that can't wait that long.
