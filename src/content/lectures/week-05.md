Full darkness is, perceptually, easier to drive in than dusk: headlights
provide a consistent contrast baseline that twilight's shifting ambient
light does not. This lecture separates three distinct failure modes —
contrast loss, glare recovery time, and fatigue — that popular accounts
collapse into one.

## Outline

- why dusk beats full night for detection difficulty
- glare recovery time as a distinct, measurable quantity
- fatigue's independent effect on reaction time
