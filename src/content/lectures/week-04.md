Impact energy scales with the square of speed, which is why a small
reduction in approach speed matters far more than instinct suggests — and
why swerving, the most common driver response, is often the worst one at
highway speed. The lecture works through the mechanics behind both claims.

## Outline

- impact energy as a function of mass and speed
- why swerving fails at speed: the geometry of the alternative
- the specific cases where late braking increases harm
- reading the week 4 modelling exercise against real recorded speeds
