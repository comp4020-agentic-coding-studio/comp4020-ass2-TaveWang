Exclusion fencing paired with underpasses cuts recorded strikes further than
almost anything else in the literature — and costs more per kilometre than a
local road authority will usually approve without an unusually strong case.
This lecture works through the evidence for each intervention class alongside
the funding case each one has to make to get built.

## Outline

- exclusion fencing and crossing structures: the strongest evidence, the
  hardest funding case
- signage: weak on its own, and why it still gets specified anyway
- virtual fencing and roadside reflectors: what the trials actually show
- matching an intervention to a road authority's actual constraints
