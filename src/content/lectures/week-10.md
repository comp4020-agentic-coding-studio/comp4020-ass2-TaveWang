International visitors and recently licensed drivers are over-represented in
strike data for reasons that have nothing to do with attention or caution —
they simply don't have the local calibration a diamond-shaped roadside sign
assumes. This lecture is about designing a risk communication that doesn't
assume calibration it hasn't earned.

## Outline

- who the standard warning sign fails, and why
- what a rental-car brief or visitor-centre handout would need to convey
- calibrating risk communication to someone with zero local reference points
- drafting the tourist-facing brief due this week
