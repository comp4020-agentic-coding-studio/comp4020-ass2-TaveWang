A map of recorded strikes is not a map of where strikes happen — it's a map
of where strikes happen *and get recorded*, and the two diverge in
predictable ways (patrol frequency, mobile coverage for reporting, road
class). This lecture works through a defensible definition of "hotspot" that
survives that distinction.

## Outline

- clustering versus reporting bias: telling them apart
- road class, traffic volume and exposure-adjusted rates
- a working definition of "hotspot" for this unit's purposes
- what the week 3 mapping exercise is actually testing
