Autonomous emergency braking systems are specified against a detection
envelope — speed, size, approach angle — and manufacturers are candid about
its edges if you read past the marketing page. This lecture reads those
specifications directly against the collision dataset to find where the
technology's coverage runs out.

## Outline

- roo bars: what they change about outcome, and what they don't
- AEB detection envelopes, read from the manufacturer's own documentation
- where the dataset's recorded strikes fall outside that envelope
