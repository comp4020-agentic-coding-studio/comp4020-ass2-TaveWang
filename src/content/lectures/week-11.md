Moose and deer strikes are a much larger literature than kangaroo strikes,
and it's tempting to import its findings wholesale — but a moose's mass and
gait, and a deer's herd behaviour, don't map cleanly onto a kangaroo's, and
some of the best-evidenced interventions from that literature transfer badly
as a result. This lecture is about telling which parts travel.

## Outline

- moose strikes in Scandinavia: mass, gait, and why AEB tuning differs
- deer strikes in North America: herd behaviour and seasonal migration
- what does and doesn't transfer to the macropod case
- reading a comparative literature without over-generalising from it
