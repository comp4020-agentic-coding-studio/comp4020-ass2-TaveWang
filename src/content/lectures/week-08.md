An animal-strike claim sits oddly across Australian motor insurance and
third-party schemes, because "no one is at fault" doesn't mean "no one pays" —
it means the payment rules shift to comprehensive cover, CTP, and in some
states a road authority's own liability, each triggered differently depending
on what the vehicle actually hit and where.

## Outline

- comprehensive versus CTP: which one an animal strike actually falls under
- road authority liability for a known, unmitigated hotspot
- how the jurisdictional variation in this unit's dataset maps to legal
  variation between states
- what an insurer's claims data does and doesn't tell you about true incidence
