Roadside procedure after a strike has two audiences with different goals:
road authorities optimise for driver and traffic safety, wildlife rescue
groups for the animal's welfare, and the advice each gives can pull in
different directions at the exact moment a driver has to decide something.
This lecture reads both sets of guidance side by side.

## Outline

- immediate safety procedure versus animal welfare procedure: where they
  align and where they don't
- reporting obligations and how underreporting shapes this unit's dataset
- what wildlife rescue groups actually attend, and why
- writing procedure that a distressed, untrained driver can follow
